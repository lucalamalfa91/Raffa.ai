## Jurisdiction notes for annotators (not legal advice)

- IT: recesso, penale (art. 1382 c.c.), clausole vessatorie (art. 1341 c.c.).
- FR: clause pénale (art. 1231-5 Code civil, judge may moderate), imprévision/révision (art. 1195).
- ES: cláusula penal (arts. 1152-1155 Código Civil, moderación judicial), tácita reconducción in leases is a distinct term (art. 1566).
- DE: Vertragsstrafe (§§ 339 ff. BGB), AGB-Kontrolle (§§ 305 ff. BGB), automatic renewal clauses in standard terms are tested for transparency (BGH XII ZR 31/17).
